Questions

Good to know.

Is MegaLab really free to start?

Yes. Create an account with Google and build your first course at no cost. Bring your own Google OAuth client ID if you self-host — the app is a static site with no vendor lock-in.

What happens with my Google login?

MegaLab uses Google Identity Services in the browser. We read your basic profile (name, email, avatar) to create your workspace. If you self-host, you can swap in your own OAuth client ID in google-client-id.js. No password ever touches MegaLab.

Which AI powers the mentor?

MegaLab is provider-agnostic. It can use a host-provided bridge (window.megalabAI) or a JSON endpoint you configure in google-client-id.js. Without either, a deterministic offline tutor keeps every workflow usable — plans, lessons, quizzes, cards, games and reviews all still work.

How does Google Classroom fit in?

Connect Google in the Classroom tab and MegaLab reads your active classes, assignments and teacher materials. Study files are downloaded only when you choose to build a pack, then PDF, PPTX, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, MD, CSV, HTML and RTF text is extracted in the browser and turned into games, questions and flashcards. You can also upload files or paste notes without connecting Google at all.

Does it work offline?

The interface and your saved courses do. Add it to your home screen: a service worker caches the app shell, so lessons, notes, cards and focus sessions keep working without a connection.

Can I export or delete my data?

Anytime. Profile → Data → Export downloads a complete JSON snapshot. Delete workspace removes it from this device immediately and deletes the Drive snapshot if Google Drive sync was connected. Drive tokens live only in this tab and expire automatically.

Is this a replacement for a teacher?

No. It's a study system that makes practice deliberate. Use it alongside classes, books and real projects — and verify important facts with primary sources.
